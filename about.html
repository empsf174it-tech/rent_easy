Our Story

From one garage to eighteen cities

Rent Easy started in 2010 with four cars, one phone line and a stubborn conviction: renting a car should not be the worst part of a trip. No queues at a counter. No upsell theatre. No mystery charges three weeks later.

A decade on we operate 120 vehicles across 18 cities, and that conviction hasn't moved. Every car in the fleet is serviced to manufacturer specification, photographed before and after each rental, and detailed by hand between guests.

The Road So Far

Milestones

Rent Easy opens in a single garage in Beverly Hills with four sedans, run entirely by its two founders. The first month closes with eleven rentals.
We add our first sports cars and introduce the concierge delivery service that has since become the way most of our guests take their keys.
A third of the fleet moves to electric and hybrid drivetrains, and every hub gets on-site fast charging. Guests pay the same rate regardless of powertrain.
120 vehicles, 18 city hubs and 34,000 completed journeys — still independently owned, still answering the phone ourselves.
